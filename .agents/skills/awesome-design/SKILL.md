# Awesome Design: World-Class Design Systems & Benchmarks

Curated design patterns from the world's most admired developer software and digital products.

## Archetype Reference Catalog

### 1. Linear Archetype (SaaS Precision & Keyboard-First)
- **Palette**: Matte Obsidian (`#08090a`), Card Surface (`#121316`), Specular border (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`).
- **Typography**: Inter/Geist with tight letter-spacing (`-0.02em`), tabular numbers (`tabular-nums`).
- **Signature**: Keyboard shortcut badges (`⌘K`), subtle hover border illumination, zero motion fluff.

### 2. Stripe Press Archetype (Academic & Editorial Gravitas)
- **Palette**: Warm Cream Canvas (`#fbfbfa`), Charcoal Ink (`#1a1918`), Accent Burnt Sienna / Indigo.
- **Typography**: Editorial Serif (`Instrument Serif`, `Newsreader`) paired with clean geometric Sans.
- **Signature**: Chapter bands, illuminated drop-caps, archival footnote sidebars.

### 3. Raycast / Apple Developer Archetype (Ergonomic Glass & Fluid Depth)
- **Palette**: Deep frosted slate (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)`), hairline 1px highlights, muted system accents.
- **Typography**: SF Pro / Plus Jakarta Sans with crisp hierarchy.
- **Signature**: High-blur backdrops (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), smooth spring-physics sheets.
