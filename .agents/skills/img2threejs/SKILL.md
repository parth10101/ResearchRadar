# Img2Three.js: Procedural 3D & Shader Synthesis Engine

Reconstruct visual designs, academic topology, and reference concepts as pure code-only Three.js models, parametric geometries, and GPU shaders.

## Core Capabilities
- **Parametric & Procedural Geometry**: Construct custom BufferGeometry, implicit Signed Distance Fields (SDF), and subdivision surfaces.
- **Physics & Material Shading**: Physically accurate PBR materials, custom ShaderMaterial uniforms, and GLSL fragment filters.
- **Reference Grimoire**: Includes complete math patterns for analysis-by-synthesis, visual hull reconstruction, and procedural rigging in `./references/`.
