# 3D Force-Directed Graph: Academic Topology & Citation Networks

High-performance WebGL graph visualization engine capable of rendering 10,000+ nodes and edges in 3D space with d3-force physics.

## Key Scientometric Features
- **3D Node Layouts**: Sphere, cluster, or topological manifold coordinates.
- **Dynamic Link Curvature & Particles**: Animate directional citation flows using link direction particles (`linkDirectionalParticles`).
- **Camera Zooming & Focus**: Smoothly fly camera to any selected publication or cluster centroid (`cameraPosition`).
- **LOD (Level of Detail)**: Switch from billboarding particle dots to 3D labels as camera approaches.
